Zombie Survival

You're a regular civilian with a pistol, and the zombies are coming. Blast them, grab their coins, find the coins hidden around the map, and buy bigger guns in the shop. Ammo never runs out!

Play together on different computers or tablets on the same Wi-Fi, up to 4 players. Everyone keeps their own coins and guns, and you shop together between waves.

Host a game

On the computer that is sharing the game. You'll get a join code to tell your friends.

Join a game

Type the 4-number code from the host's screen.

Hosting only works on the computer that is sharing the game. On this device you join.

To play on Wi-Fi, double-click share-on-network.command in the game's folder first. Then everyone opens the address it shows (it starts with http:// and ends with :8010) in their web browser. The host opens http://localhost:8010 on the sharing computer.
