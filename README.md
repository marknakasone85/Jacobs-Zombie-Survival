# Jacob's Zombie Survival

<h2 align="center"><a href="https://marknakasone85.github.io/Jacobs-Zombie-Survival/">▶ PLAY NOW</a></h2>
<p align="center">Free in your web browser, on any computer, tablet or phone. Nothing to download.</p>

A side-scrolling zombie shooter. You're a regular civilian with a pistol and the zombies are coming.
Blast them, grab the coins they drop, find the coins hidden around each map, and buy bigger guns in the shop.
Ammo never runs out. (made by a 9 year old with Claude Code)

## Play it

- **In your web browser:** https://marknakasone85.github.io/Jacobs-Zombie-Survival/ (1 or 2 players on one device).
- **On your own computer:** download the game (the green **Code** button, then **Download ZIP**), unzip it and double-click `index.html`. There's nothing to install. Every picture and sound is made in code.
- **With friends on your home Wi-Fi (2 to 4 players):** see [Playing together on Wi-Fi](#playing-together-on-wi-fi-2-to-4-players) below.

Your coins, guns and levels are saved in the web browser you play in.

The code is on GitHub: https://github.com/marknakasone85/Jacobs-Zombie-Survival

## How it works

- **Levels and waves:** 13 levels, and every level has its own series of waves that get bigger and harder (3 waves on the first map, up to 6 on the last ones), and its own **boss**. Between waves the Gun Shop opens. Beat the last wave to unlock the next level. The later levels are HARD: buy better guns, use the platforms, and bring friends. Beat the last level and a rescue helicopter comes for you: **YOU SURVIVED!**
- **Kill counter:** next to the wave number, a little skull shows how many zombies you've beaten out of everything the level sends, like **37/120** (bosses included). It turns green when you've got them all.

  | 3 | Graveyard (rainy night, ground fog) | zombie crows, plus the boss: **The Grave Giant** (4 waves) |
  | 4 | Frozen Forest (snowy night, northern lights) | swarms of tiny zombies, zombie wolves, bears, a giant, and the boss: **The Yeti** (5 waves) |

- **Zombies come in different sizes:** walkers, runners, leapers, brutes, **tiny** zombies (small and fast), **giants** (huge and slow), and **bloaters**. Bloaters pop in a goo explosion when they die: it hurts other zombies, and you if you're standing too close! Every zombie is also a bit bigger or smaller than the last, and bigger ones are tougher.
- **Zombie animals** (each with its own sounds):
  - **Dogs** are fast and pounce at you.
  - **Sheep** paw the ground, then CHARGE straight ahead and send you flying. They can't turn while charging, so jump over them!
  - **Kangaroos** hop everywhere, even up onto platforms, and punch with their boxing gloves.
  - **Crows** fly around above you and dive down to peck.
  - **Bears** are huge and tough. They rear up and swipe.
  - Bosses: **The Big Bad Ram** charges, **King Roo** shakes the ground when it lands, and **Big Grizz** stomps and swipes.
- **Every level has a boss** in its last wave (some scary, some funny):
  - **Mr. Mower** (Maple Street): a grumpy zombie dad in a pink bathrobe and fuzzy slippers on a ride-on lawn mower. He revs up, then charges across the street spraying grass. Jump over him!
  - **The Rat King** (Downtown): a giant sewer rat with a little gold crown. Watch out for its huge pounces.
  - **The Grave Giant** (Graveyard), **General Rotten** (Army Base) and **The Big Top Brute** (Carnival): huge zombies that stomp the ground.
  - **The Yeti** (Frozen Forest): throws giant snowballs at you and stomps.
  - **King Bloat** (Desert Gas Stop): spits goo and belly-flops. When he pops, four little bloaters burst out of him!
  - **The Big Bad Ram**, **King Roo** and **Big Grizz** (the farm, the outback and the zoo), **The Sewer Gator** (New York City), a giant zombie alligator that opens its jaws wide, then lunges (jump over it!), **The Storm Crow** (Sky Escape), a giant zombie crow that circles your helicopter, and **The Road Hulk**, a huge road-warrior zombie with a red mohawk, goggles and a spiked shoulder pad.
- **Coins:** zombies drop coins (big zombies drop more), and coins get sucked into you like a **black hole**. They spiral around faster and faster until you swallow them, with a golden swirl around you. Coins dropped by zombies come from anywhere on the map. Coins lying around the map (on cars, sheds, scaffolds, crypts and so on, plus shiny **10-coins** in the hardest spots) start flying to you once you get close. More coins appear each wave. You keep your coins and weapons between games.
- **Hearts:** zombies sometimes drop a heart (+25 health). You also heal 25 after every wave.
- **Platforms:** jump up through cars, sheds, the bus, scaffolds and crypts and stand on top. Press Down to drop off. Most zombies can only jump onto low ones, but leapers can reach you anywhere.

## Weapons

Ammo never runs out. Melee weapons hit every zombie right in front of you in one swing.
Damage numbers add up: keep hitting the same zombie and its number counts up (it turns gold at 50 and orange at 200). A really big hit (200 or more, like a Sniper shot, a rocket or a High Explosive grenade) freezes the action for a split second, so you feel it.
You start with a plain 9mm pistol: save up after the first wave for the Pump Shotgun. Don't like a gun you bought? **Sell it back** in the Gun Shop and you get all your coins back (you can't sell the starter pistol).

## Grenades

Throw them with **G**, a **right-click** (it throws at the mouse), or the grenade button on phones and tablets. **R** (or the small button next to it) switches the kind you throw. Buy them in the Gun Shop. Each one is used up when you throw it, and you can carry up to 10 of each kind. Everyone starts with 3 frag grenades.

- A grenade that hits a zombie in the air **blows up right away**.
- One that hits the ground **bounces and rolls**, then **sticks** to the first zombie it touches and blows up a moment later. If no zombie comes, it blows up by itself.

| Grenade | Price (each) | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Frag Grenade | 15 | a boom, plus sharp metal pieces that fly out and hit zombies further away |
| High Explosive | 30 | the biggest boom in the shop (480 damage) |
| Acid Grenade | 25 | a bubbling acid puddle that melts every zombie walking through it for 6 seconds |
| Cryo Grenade | 25 | freezes zombies solid for 3.5 seconds (bosses shake it off sooner). Frozen zombies can't walk or bite, crows fall out of the sky, and frozen zombies take 50% more damage |

Your grenades show at the bottom of your player panel. The kind you'll throw next is lit up.

## The helicopter level (Sky Escape)

Everyone flies their own helicopter and stands in its open side door with all their guns. The controls are the same:

- **Left / right** flies, **jump** goes up, **down** goes down. Let go and it hovers.
- **Shoot** fires your gun: in front, behind, or straight down at the zombies below.
- Fly **low and fast** to chop zombies (and crows) with the rotor.
- Zombie crows chase you in the air, and kangaroos and leapers jump up at you if you fly low. Coins float high up in the sky: fly up to grab them.

## The driving level (Highway Escape)

On the last level everyone drives their own big pickup truck, with spikes on the front, and stands in the back with all their guns. The controls are the same:

- **Left / right** drives (the truck turns round when you change direction). It's much faster than running.
- **Jump** makes the truck hop: over a crowd, or up onto a wrecked bus.
- **Shoot** fires your gun. Auto-aim looks **both ways**, so you can drive forward and shoot the zombies chasing you.
- **Drive into zombies** to run them over: the faster you go, the further they fly, and the truck keeps its speed. Brutes and giants get plowed out of the way but slow you down. Bosses stop your truck dead, so shoot those.
- Driving into a barrel sets it off!

**The twist:** when the Road Hulk goes down... **"IT'S NOT OVER!"** More giant bosses and a flood of zombies come at you from both sides. Hang on (you can't drop below 1 health while it lasts): **"AIR SUPPORT INCOMING!"** Two fighter jets roar past, a stealth bomber drops a carpet of bombs on both sides of you, and two attack gunship helicopters fly in and shoot whatever is left. Then the military rescue helicopter comes down, everybody climbs its rope ladder, and it flies you out of Zombie City: **YOU SURVIVED!** You can watch the ending again from the end of the level list.

## Barrels

Every map has a few barrels and tanks standing around. Shoot one, hit it with a sword or hammer, burn it, or blow it up, and it goes off. Wait until zombies are crowding around it! Explosions set off the barrels close by too, for a **CHAIN REACTION**. The electric barrel's lightning sets them off as well. Each one you shoot comes back 30 seconds later (it drops in from the sky), and they are all back for every new wave. Careful: the explosive ones hurt you a bit if you stand right next to them.

| Barrel | What it does |
|---|---|
| Explosive Barrel (red, with a flame sign) | a huge boom (340 damage) |
| Oil Drum (black, OIL) | a fireball, then burning oil on the ground for 7 seconds |
| Toxic Barrel (yellow, with a toxic sign) | a wide puddle of acid that melts zombies for 8 seconds |
| Sticky Slime Barrel (purple, pink slime) | pink slime everywhere for 10 seconds: zombies stuck in it can only wade along very slowly, and can't jump, hop or charge |
| Liquid Nitrogen Tank (light blue, snowflake) | freezes every zombie close by for 4.5 seconds |
| Gas Tank (white, GAS) | the valve breaks and it **rockets off**, spinning, then blows up on the first zombie it hits. Shoot it in the air to blow it up early! |
| Electric Barrel (a giant battery) | lightning jumps from zombie to zombie, up to 12 of them, and stuns them for 2.5 seconds |

Shot barrels leak (fire, gas, oil, acid, slime or sparks), so you can see which ones are about to go.

## Armor and health

Also in the Gun Shop. Your armor shows as a **blue ARMOR bar** under your health. It takes the hits before your health does, and it's repaired at the start of every wave. You wear one set at a time (you can own several and switch), and armor can be sold back for all your coins too.

| Armor | Price | Armor bar |
|---|---|---|
| Leather Jacket (and cool shades) | 60 | 25 |
| Riot Gear (helmet with visor, chest plate, knee pads) | 220 | 60 |
| Full Military (combat helmet, camo, heavy vest) | 480 | 120 |
| Space Suit (glass bubble helmet, backpack, blinking lights) | 950 | 250 |

- **Double Health** (500 coins) makes your health bar 200 instead of 100, for good. After that, **Triple Health** (1000 coins) makes it 300.
- The **Med Kit** (25 coins) heals 50 between waves.
- In 2-player and Wi-Fi games everyone buys their own armor and health with their own coins.

## Controls

**1 player**

| Keys | Action |
|---|---|
| ← → or A D | move |
| ↑ or W | jump |
| ↓ or S | drop off a platform |
| Space, or click | shoot (clicking aims at the mouse, Space aims at the nearest zombie in front of you) |
| hold Space and walk away | walk backwards while shooting |
| 1–9 and 0, Q E, or the mouse wheel | switch weapon |
| G, or right-click | throw a grenade (right-click throws it at the mouse) |
| R | switch grenade type |
| P | pause |
| M | sound on/off |

**2 players on one keyboard.** Every player has their own coins and guns, and you shop together on the same screen. Each gun has a P1 and a P2 button.

| | Move / jump / drop | Shoot | Switch gun | Grenade / switch grenade |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | A D / W / S | F (or click) | Q E, 1–9 and 0 | G / R (or right-click) |
| P2 | ← → / ↑ / ↓ | Enter or / | , . | Right Shift / ' |

If one player goes down, they pop back in next to their friend after 6 seconds. Game over only happens if everyone is down.

**Phones and tablets:** on-screen buttons appear in 1-player and Wi-Fi games (◀ ▶ move, ▼ drop down, JUMP, FIRE, ⇄ switch gun, the grenade button to throw and the small one next to it to switch grenade type, II menu). Hold phones sideways. Aiming is automatic.

**Game controllers** (Xbox, PlayStation, Switch Pro and others): plug one in or connect it by Bluetooth, then press any button so the browser notices it. A controller line appears under the keys on the start screen.

| Button | Action |
|---|---|
| left stick or D-pad | move (down drops off a platform) |
| A, or D-pad up | jump |
| RT or X | shoot |
| LT or B | throw a grenade |
| Y | switch grenade type |
| LB / RB | switch gun |
| Start | pause |

On a PlayStation controller A is ✕, B is ◯, X is □, Y is △, LB/RB are L1/R1 and LT/RT are L2/R2.
- 1 player: any controller. 2 players: controller 1 is P1 and controller 2 is P2. With just one controller it's P2's, and P1 uses the keyboard.
- The controller buzzes when you get hurt and on really big hits (if the controller and browser can buzz).
- In Wi-Fi games every player can use a controller on their own device. Some browsers only allow controllers on secure (https) pages, so on friends' devices a controller may not work through the Wi-Fi share address. It always works on the public web page.

## Playing together on Wi-Fi (2 to 4 players)

Everyone plays on their own computer, tablet or phone, on the same home Wi-Fi. Up to 4 players fight the waves and the whole campaign together.

1. **On the host computer** (the one with the game folder): double-click **`share-on-network.command`**. A window opens with this computer's Wi-Fi address, like `http://<wifi-network-address>:8010`. Keep it open while you play.
   - The first time, macOS may ask whether Python can accept incoming network connections. Click **Allow**, or nobody else can connect.
2. **Host:** open **http://localhost:8010** on that computer, press **WI-FI**, then **HOST A GAME**. A 4-number **join code** appears.
   - Host in **one window only**. If you press HOST A GAME in a second window, the first one stops (it says so), and friends need the new code.
3. **Everyone else:** open the address from the sharing window (it looks like `http://<wifi-network-address>:8010`) in a web browser, press **WI-FI**, type the code and press **JOIN**. (Other devices only see JOIN: hosting only works on the sharing computer.)
   - **Phones:** use Safari on an iPhone (Chrome on an iPhone has to be allowed "Local Network" in the iPhone's Settings first). Turn the phone sideways.
4. The host picks a level and everyone plays together on one shared screen. Friends can join in the middle of a level too.
   - More players means more zombies in every wave, so 4 friends still get a real fight.

- **Your own coins and guns:** every player keeps their own wallet and guns on their own device. Coins you grab in a Wi-Fi game are saved on your device, and levels you beat together unlock for you too.
- **Shopping together:** between waves everyone sees the Gun Shop and everybody's wallets. You buy with your own coins, see which guns your friends have, and press **I'M READY**. The host starts the next wave.
- **P** opens a menu. Wi-Fi games never pause, because your friends are still playing!
- The join code is always shown at the bottom left of the screen.
- If someone's connection drops, the game tries to reconnect by itself. A phone that locks its screen or switches apps for a moment stays in the game. A friend who's gone for 25 seconds is taken out of the game and can rejoin with the same code.
- The sharing window switches off after 3 hours. If friends suddenly can't connect, start `share-on-network.command` again.

How it works: the host's computer runs the real game. The others send their button presses and draw what the host sends back, about 25 updates a second, roughly 1-4 KB each. The host's game keeps running even if its window is hidden or in a background tab.

Security: only the game files are shared, read-only. Only the host's own computer can start a game, joining needs the code, a device that types too many wrong codes is locked out for a minute, other websites can't talk to the game, and trick web addresses can't reach any other file. It's home-network only and switches itself off after 3 hours. Only use it on your own home Wi-Fi, not public Wi-Fi.

## Adding a level

Open `js/levels.js` and copy one of the entries in `LEVELS`. The comment at the top explains every field (theme, platforms, coins, barrels, waves, boss). The farm, outback and zoo backgrounds and their platforms are in `js/themes.js`.

## About

A family project, made for Jacob and his friends. The code was written with Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic.

Content: cartoon fighting against made-up zombies and zombie animals, with cartoon and sci-fi weapons. There's no real-world violence and no real people in it. All characters, places and weapons are made up and drawn in code, and the game isn't connected with any company, game or brand.

## Privacy

The game collects no personal information. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
- Coins, guns and unlocked levels are saved only in your own web browser and never leave your device.
- The fonts come with the game, so playing never contacts another website. A security setting in the page (a Content Security Policy) also tells the browser to refuse anything from another website: no outside scripts, pictures, fonts or connections.
- Wi-Fi games stay on your home network. Button presses and game updates go between the players' devices through the host computer and are kept in memory only.
- The public web page is hosted by GitHub Pages, which keeps the usual web server logs (see GitHub's privacy statement).
