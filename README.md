# Jacob's Zombie Survival

A side-scrolling zombie shooter. You're a regular civilian with a shotgun and the zombies are coming.
Blast them, grab the coins they drop, find the coins hidden around each map, and buy bigger guns in the shop.
Ammo never runs out. (made by a 9 year old with Claude Code)

## Play it

**Play online:** https://marknakasone85.github.io/Jacobs-Zombie-Survival/ (1 or 2 players on one device, on any computer, tablet or phone).

Double-click `index.html`. There's nothing to install. Every picture and sound is made in code.

## How it works

- **Levels and waves:** 10 levels, and every level has its own series of waves that get bigger and harder (3 waves on the first map, up to 6 on the last ones). Between waves the Gun Shop opens. Beat the last wave to unlock the next level. The later levels are HARD: buy better guns, use the platforms, and bring friends.

  | # | Level | What's new |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 | Maple Street (suburbs at sunset) | walkers, runners, the first zombie dogs (3 waves) |
  | 2 | Downtown (city at night) | leapers that jump onto platforms, brutes, stray zombie dogs (4 waves) |
  | 3 | Graveyard | zombie crows, plus the boss: **The Grave Giant** (4 waves) |
  | 4 | Frozen Forest (snowy night, northern lights) | swarms of tiny zombies, zombie wolves, bears, a giant (5 waves) |
  | 5 | Desert Gas Stop (sunset, cacti, tumbleweeds) | bloaters, giants, zombie vultures (5 waves) |
  | 6 | Army Base (searchlights, tanks, watchtowers) | army dogs, plus the boss: **General Rotten** (6 waves) |
  | 7 | Creepy Carnival (Ferris wheel, circus tents) | boxing kangaroos, the petting zoo sheep, and the boss: **The Big Top Brute** (6 waves) |
  | 8 | Zombie Farm (sunny morning, barn, cornfield) | dogs, charging sheep, crows, a bear, and the boss: **The Big Bad Ram** (5 waves) |
  | 9 | The Outback (red dirt, gum trees, a giant red rock) | kangaroos everywhere, and the boss: **King Roo** (5 waves) |
  | 10 | Zombie Zoo (moonlit zoo, open cages) | every animal at once, and the final boss: **Big Grizz** (6 waves) |

- **Zombies come in different sizes:** walkers, runners, leapers, brutes, **tiny** zombies (small and fast), **giants** (huge and slow), and **bloaters**. Bloaters pop in a goo explosion when they die: it hurts other zombies, and you if you're standing too close! Every zombie is also a bit bigger or smaller than the last, and bigger ones are tougher.
- **Zombie animals** (each with its own sounds):
  - **Dogs** are fast and pounce at you.
  - **Sheep** paw the ground, then CHARGE straight ahead and send you flying. They can't turn while charging, so jump over them!
  - **Kangaroos** hop everywhere, even up onto platforms, and punch with their boxing gloves.
  - **Crows** fly around above you and dive down to peck.
  - **Bears** are huge and tough. They rear up and swipe.
  - Bosses: **The Big Bad Ram** charges, **King Roo** shakes the ground when it lands, and **Big Grizz** stomps and swipes.
- **Coins:** zombies drop coins (big zombies drop more), and coins get sucked into you like a **black hole**. They spiral around faster and faster until you swallow them, with a golden swirl around you. Coins dropped by zombies come from anywhere on the map. Coins lying around the map (on cars, sheds, scaffolds, crypts and so on, plus shiny **10-coins** in the hardest spots) start flying to you once you get close. More coins appear each wave. You keep your coins and weapons between games.
- **Hearts:** zombies sometimes drop a heart (+25 health). You also heal 25 after every wave.
- **Platforms:** jump up through cars, sheds, the bus, scaffolds and crypts and stand on top. Press Down to drop off. Most zombies can only jump onto low ones, but leapers can reach you anywhere.

## Armor and health

Also in the Gun Shop. Your armor shows as a **blue ARMOR bar** under your health. It takes the hits before your health does, and it's repaired at the start of every wave. You wear one set at a time (you can own several and switch), and armor can be sold back for all your coins too.

| Armor | Price | Armor bar |
|---|---|---|
| Leather Jacket (and cool shades) | 60 | 25 |
| Riot Gear (helmet with visor, chest plate, knee pads) | 220 | 60 |
| Full Military (combat helmet, camo, heavy vest) | 480 | 120 |
| Space Suit (glass bubble helmet, backpack, blinking lights) | 950 | 250 |

- **Double Health** (500 coins) makes your health bar 200 instead of 100, for good. After that, **Triple Health** (1000 coins) makes it 300.
- The **Med Kit** (25 coins) heals 50 between waves.
- In 2-player and Wi-Fi games everyone buys their own armor and health with their own coins.

## Controls

**1 player**

| Keys | Action |
|---|---|
| ← → or A D | move |
| ↑ or W | jump |
| ↓ or S | drop off a platform |
| Space, or click | shoot (clicking aims at the mouse, Space aims at the nearest zombie in front of you) |
| hold Space and walk away | walk backwards while shooting |
| 1–9 and 0, Q E, or the mouse wheel | switch weapon |
| P | pause |
| M | sound on/off |

**2 players on one keyboard.** Every player has their own coins and guns, and you shop together on the same screen. Each gun has a P1 and a P2 button.

| | Move / jump / drop | Shoot | Switch gun |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | A D / W / S | F (or click) | Q E, 1–9 and 0 |
| P2 | ← → / ↑ / ↓ | Enter or / | , . |

If one player goes down, they pop back in next to their friend after 6 seconds. Game over only happens if everyone is down.

**Phones and tablets:** on-screen buttons appear in 1-player and Wi-Fi games (◀ ▶ move, ▼ drop down, JUMP, FIRE, ⇄ switch gun, II menu). Hold phones sideways. Aiming is automatic.

## Playing together on Wi-Fi (2 to 4 players)

Everyone plays on their own computer, tablet or phone, on the same home Wi-Fi. Up to 4 players fight the waves and the whole campaign together.

1. **On the host computer** (the one with the game folder): double-click **`share-on-network.command`**. A window opens with this computer's Wi-Fi address, like `http://<wifi-network-address>:8010`. Keep it open while you play.
   - The first time, macOS may ask whether Python can accept incoming network connections. Click **Allow**, or nobody else can connect.
2. **Host:** open **http://localhost:8010** on that computer, press **WI-FI**, then **HOST A GAME**. A 4-number **join code** appears.
   - Host in **one window only**. If you press HOST A GAME in a second window, the first one stops (it says so), and friends need the new code.
3. **Everyone else:** open the address from the sharing window (it looks like `http://<wifi-network-address>:8010`) in a web browser, press **WI-FI**, type the code and press **JOIN**. (Other devices only see JOIN: hosting only works on the sharing computer.)
   - **Phones:** use Safari on an iPhone (Chrome on an iPhone has to be allowed "Local Network" in the iPhone's Settings first). Turn the phone sideways.
4. The host picks a level and everyone plays together on one shared screen. Friends can join in the middle of a level too.
   - More players means more zombies in every wave, so 4 friends still get a real fight.

- **Your own coins and guns:** every player keeps their own wallet and guns on their own device. Coins you grab in a Wi-Fi game are saved on your device, and levels you beat together unlock for you too.
- **Shopping together:** between waves everyone sees the Gun Shop and everybody's wallets. You buy with your own coins, see which guns your friends have, and press **I'M READY**. The host starts the next wave.
- **P** opens a menu. Wi-Fi games never pause, because your friends are still playing!
- The join code is always shown at the bottom left of the screen.
- If someone's connection drops, the game tries to reconnect by itself. A phone that locks its screen or switches apps for a moment stays in the game. A friend who's gone for 25 seconds is taken out of the game and can rejoin with the same code.
- The sharing window switches off after 3 hours. If friends suddenly can't connect, start `share-on-network.command` again.

How it works: the host's computer runs the real game. The others send their button presses and draw what the host sends back, about 25 updates a second, roughly 1-4 KB each. The host's game keeps running even if its window is hidden or in a background tab.

Security: only the game files are shared, read-only. Only the host's own computer can start a game, joining needs the code, a device that types too many wrong codes is locked out for a minute, other websites can't talk to the game, and trick web addresses can't reach any other file. It's home-network only and switches itself off after 3 hours. Only use it on your own home Wi-Fi, not public Wi-Fi.

## Adding a level

Open `js/levels.js` and copy one of the entries in `LEVELS`. The comment at the top explains every field (theme, platforms, coins, waves, boss). The farm, outback and zoo backgrounds and their platforms are in `js/themes.js`.

## About

A family project, made for Jacob and his friends. The code was written with Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic.

Content: cartoon fighting against made-up zombies and zombie animals, with cartoon and sci-fi weapons. There's no real-world violence and no real people in it. All characters, places and weapons are made up and drawn in code, and the game isn't connected with any company, game or brand.

## Privacy

The game collects no personal information. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
- Coins, guns and unlocked levels are saved only in your own web browser and never leave your device.
- The fonts come with the game, so playing never contacts another website.
- Wi-Fi games stay on your home network. Button presses and game updates go between the players' devices through the host computer and are kept in memory only.
- The public web page is hosted by GitHub Pages, which keeps the usual web server logs (see GitHub's privacy statement).
